Click a message and the panel jumps to its turn. Click a turn and the stream scrolls to it. Either way, one tap gets you back to live.

  1. 1
    Scope control NewSegmented "This turn | Session" under the tabs. The choice is remembered per tab for the session, so Plan can sit on Session while Activity follows turns. Goal ignores it (see Board D).Data: panel.scope[tab] (client state)
  2. 2
    Turn rail NewOne chip per turn with its start time, scrolls sideways past about six turns. The live turn pulses and is the default while it runs; when the session goes idle the last turn stays selected. Hidden when scope is Session.Data: turn.id, turn.startedAt, turn.status
  3. 3
    Stream and panel share the selection NewClicking any message in the stream selects its turn in the rail and outlines the turn's messages. Clicking a turn chip scrolls the stream to the turn's first message. Every event needs to carry the turn it belongs to.Data: message.turnId, event.turnId
  4. 4
    Back to live NewShows when a past turn is selected while another turn is running. Reselects the live turn and scrolls the stream to the bottom. The panel never jumps away from a turn the user picked on its own.Data: session.liveTurnId
  5. 5
    The rule NewPer-turn data is a slice: only events tagged with that turn. Session data is either a sum (tokens, time, counts) or a history (plans, goal changes, file edits). Nothing replaces anything; old versions stay, labelled with the turn that retired them.Data: all panel feeds keyed by turnId
