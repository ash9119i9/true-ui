Live requests and the composer

A pending request stops the turn until someone answers, so it can't live in the scroll where it's easy to miss. It docks above the composer, the stream keeps a quiet placeholder that later becomes the audit row, and the topbar and dock both say the turn is waiting on you. The composer gains slash commands, an approval mode and Steer. Model settings move to the agent popover.
