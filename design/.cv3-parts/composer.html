Everything that shapes the next message sits on one bar: files, agent, model and effort, approval mode and Goal. Mentions and slash commands open above the text. While a turn runs, typed messages queue in plain sight, and steering the live turn is its own button.

Goal mode with a mistyped budget. "50m" is caught before send, and an existing goal asks before it's replaced.

  1. 1
    Goal header dataExisting copy and cancel button. The box border tints and the placeholder swaps to the goal prompt.
  2. 2
    BudgetField dataPresets No budget, 25k, 100k, 250k, or Custom. Tokens only: /^(\d[\d,_]*)(k?)$/i, 1 to 10M, 12 characters max. The error shows after blur, and Start goal stays disabled while invalid.
  3. 3
    Replace-goal confirm derive newOne goal per chat. If thread/goal/get returns an active or paused goal, confirm before thread/goal/set overwrites it. Shows the old objective and its usage.

    Esc exits goal mode deriveSecond in the Esc order: menus close first, then goal mode, then popovers, then the turn stops. The draft text is kept when leaving goal mode.
