Overview

The page at a glance

One rule decides placement. Content (messages, work, answers and live requests) is inline in the stream. Status (turn state, goal, helpers) is pinned to the topbar, the goal strip and the session panel. Diagnostics (traces, tokens, limits) stay folded behind the receipt and the Insights tab. One derived turn state feeds every status surface, so they never disagree.

One function, one answer. The pill, chip, badge and count read the same derived state.

  1. 1
    One deriveTurnState() deriveReads running, busy, status, pending, goal and stream state and returns one of eight states. Replaces the ad hoc stateText chain.
  2. 2
    Starting derivebusy is true and no turn is running yet. Today this gap shows "ready" while the send is in flight.
  3. 3
    Waiting on you deriveAny open PendingRequest. Beats Working so a blocked turn never looks busy. The helper chip names who is asking.
  4. 4
    Stopping deriveSet locally when Stop pauses the goal and calls interrupt; cleared on turn.completed. A 409 means the turn already ended.
  5. 5
    Existing states dataReady, working, working on goal, retrying and reconnecting map one to one onto today's stateText. Failed reads TurnFailure.reason for its copy.
  6. 6
    Reconnecting freezes counts dataWhile the stream resyncs, badge and count hold their last value instead of dropping to zero, then snapshot hydration updates them.
