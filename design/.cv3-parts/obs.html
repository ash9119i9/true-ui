Receipts and insights

Say what happened first, show the numbers on request

Every finished turn gets a receipt that reads like a sentence: what the agent did, how long it took, what it used, and anything that needs the user's hand. Developer mode unlocks the waterfall, token stack and context ring. Privacy findings become actions, rate limits become turns left, and the Insights tab answers "where does the time go" for the whole session.
