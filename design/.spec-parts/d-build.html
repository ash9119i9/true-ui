Accessibility

The prototype already covers most of the basics. Keep all of it and close the gaps listed below. Full WCAG validation still needs manual testing with a screen reader (VoiceOver, NVDA) and an accessibility review. This list does not replace that.

Already in the prototype

  • Every input has a <label for>. The name field has autocomplete="name", maxlength="60" and required.
  • Invalid submit sets aria-invalid="true" on the name input, shows #nameErr (linked with aria-describedby) and moves focus back to the input.
  • The progress bar has role="progressbar", aria-label="Join progress", aria-valuemin="0", aria-valuemax="3", and aria-valuenow is updated on every step.
  • Submit phases and results are announced through a visually hidden aria-live="polite" paragraph (#live).
  • The form is labelled by its eyebrow (aria-labelledby="join-sec-h"). The capabilities panel is an <aside> labelled by its h2.
  • The board is aria-hidden="true". The legend is the readable version of it.
  • The verified badge SVG has aria-label="Verified admin", and its wrapper has title="Workspace admin".
  • :focus-visible ring: 2px solid --accent-2, offset 2px, radius 8px.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce cuts every animation and transition to .01ms, and the parallax listener is never attached.

Build plan

Verified in the repo: the app is a single file, app.tsx, rendered by index.html with React UMD and in-browser Babel. Theme uses useTheme() with localStorage["truex-theme"], and tokens live in the :root block of index.html. I did not find or verify a router. The route, API and analytics below are proposals.

1. Tokens

  • Add to index.html :root: the agent tints (--ice --ice-dot --mint --mint-dot --butter --butter-dot --peach --peach-dot --lilac --lilac-dot --tint-ink). They are fixed hex values with no dark variant.
  • Add the board-only tokens with both light and dark values: --board-bg --board-dot --glass --glass-hi --glass-line --sheen --obj-shadow --obj-drop --noise. Values are in Color tokens.
  • Add the missing dark --shadow-sm (0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 30%)) to both dark blocks.

The three submit phase labels ("Verifying your invite", "Setting up your seat", "Opening your first conversation") are a fake 3 × 700ms sequence in the prototype. In production, show them while the real request is in flight: advance one label every 700ms, stop on the last label until the response arrives, and never keep the user waiting just to finish the animation.

4. Route, data and events (proposals)

  • Route: /join/:token. Signed-out users sign in first and return to this URL.
  • Data: GET the invite by token, POST accept with { name }, and POST request-new-invite. The payload shape is in Logic & wiring.
  • Analytics: invite_viewed (with state), invite_name_entered (first valid name), invite_joined, invite_join_failed, invite_expired_request.
  • Remove before shipping: the theme segmented control, the Preview state footer and the ?theme= query parameter.

5. Order of work

  1. Tokens in index.html.
  2. Left pane and the state machine with a mocked invite. Ship-ready on its own.
  3. Board materials (clover, glass, grain, lit, drop), then specimens 01 to 08, then the legend.
  4. Wire the real invite data (names, counts, expiry, teammate photo).
  5. Motion pause, reduced motion, accessibility fixes, QA.

QA and acceptance

Visual

  • At 1440×900 and 1280×760 the page has no scrollbar. The left pane, the board and the full legend fit in one screen.
  • Left content starts near the top: the inviter row sits about 7vh below the header (clamped 20 to 72px), not vertically centred.
  • Legend titles stay on one line, level with their 01 to 08 numbers. Descriptions wrap to 2 lines at most.
  • The legend has 4 columns at 1400px and above, 2 columns from 521 to 1399px, and 1 column at 520px and below.
  • Board specimens never overlap each other's labels at any desktop size. The board shrinks with the window height.
  • Light and dark both match the reference screenshots. Auto follows the OS setting.
  • At 400px wide the layout stacks with a 16px gutter and no horizontal scroll, and the board uses the 5 : 6.4 ratio.

Behaviour

  • Typing a name updates the input avatar, step 2, the progress bar (2 of 3), the hint ("looks good") and specimen 07 on every keystroke.
  • Submitting an empty or 1-character name shows the error, sets aria-invalid and focuses the field.
  • A valid submit shows the spinner and the phase labels, then the "You're in" banner, "Open Northwind Labs", 3 of 3, and 07 with a check and "joined just now".
  • Expired: red pill "Expired Sep 23", warning banner, "Request a new invite". After clicking, the success banner shows and the button is disabled.
  • Already a member: all steps done, "Open Northwind Labs", 07 shows Alex Rivera "member since Aug".
  • Hovering or focusing a legend item dims the other specimens to .3 and scales the matching one to 1.04.
  • With reduced motion on, nothing loops, there is no parallax, and the highlight still works.
  • The whole flow can be completed with the keyboard only.
