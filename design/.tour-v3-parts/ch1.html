Step 4 of 12 · First look

Choose what waits for you

Ask checks with you before each tool. Auto-allow read-only lets tools that only read data run on their own. Autopilot runs tools without asking, and only shows when your workspace turns it on.

Plans and secrets always ask, in every mode.
