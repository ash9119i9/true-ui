Team · 3 of 4

Pins tell you who is here

A blue pin means the agent is on your team. The red pin marks the agent in this chat. Built-ins show a lock: you can use them, not change them. Click a card to chat.

BackNext
3

Every card has a pin. The red one is the agent you are talking to right now.

  1. 1
    Step: a pinned card Look deriveCopy: "Pins tell you who is here. A blue pin means the agent is on your team. The red pin marks the agent in this chat. Built-ins show a lock: you can use them, not change them. Click a card to chat."
  2. 2
    Target and prepare() derivedata-tour="team-card" goes on the card whose agent is in the current chat (Qlik Analyst here). With no current chat, it goes on the first card and the red pin sentence drops. prepare() scrolls the card into view and waits for the pins overlay to measure.
  3. 3
    Advance and fallback deriveNext, Enter or → advances. A click on the card starts a chat and minimizes the tour to the resume chip. If team-card is missing after 3s, the copy shows as a centered card.
  4. 4
    Card data dataFrame is the tint dot, photo is the tint bg, handle, description, model and effort come from GET /api/agents. builtIn drives the lock note. The in chat badge compares the card to the session's agent.
  5. 5
    Edit and delete, later backendNeed PATCH /api/agents/:id and DELETE /api/agents/:id. Until they ship, the tour never points at edit or delete and the copy does not mention them.

4

The empty frame at the end is the door to the Create tour.

  1. 1
    Step: new agent Look deriveCopy: "Add your own agent. New agent opens a short dialog: a name, a look and a model. Next opens it and the Create tour walks you through." Coach left of the card on wide screens.
  2. 2
    Target and prepare() derivedata-tour="new-agent" on the dashed New agent card, the last item on the board. prepare() clears search and filter so the card is rendered, then scrolls it into view. The header New agent button opens the same dialog but is not a target.
  3. 3
    Advance and hand-off deriveNext, Enter or a click on the card opens AgentDialog. The team tour sets parts.team and, once the dialog is open, starts the Create tour at "Create · 1 of 5" on agent-name.
  4. 4
    Fallback deriveIf new-agent is missing after 3s, the copy shows as a centered card whose Next still opens the dialog. If the dialog never opens, the tour ends here and parts.create stays unset.
  5. 5
    Data dataCreate uses POST /api/agents, which exists today. Edit and delete stay out of this tour until PATCH and DELETE /api/agents/:id ship.
