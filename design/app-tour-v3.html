The first ten minutes, guided

The guided tour rebuilt on the Conversation v3 design. A soft spotlight walks a new user through the sidebar, the composer's action bar, one real request with its helpers, the needs-you tray and the goal strip. Two short agent tours cover the team page and creating an agent. Every stop says whether its data exists today, is derived on the client, or waits on the backend.

Five states and two flags. Nothing ends the tour by accident: Esc and close always land on Minimized.

  1. 1
    Entry deriveAuto-starts once, on the first empty hero after sign-in. A key with v below 3 counts as not started, but its done value picks the What's new path.
  2. 2
    Running derivestep is written on every Next and Back. The tour key handler runs in the capture phase, so the v3 Esc order (menu, goal mode, popover, stop turn) only runs when no coach is open.
  3. 3
    Finished Look deriveThe only card where Esc closes. It spots tour-replay under Account and offers "Meet your team" to start the Team tour.
  4. 4
    Skipped deriveSkip tour sits on the Welcome card. "Skip this" on a Try stop only skips that stop. The step is kept so Replay can offer to continue.
  5. 5
    Minimized deriveRunning is in memory only, so a reload with done:false shows the chip, never a coach. The chip reopens at step and reruns its prepare(). A Wait stop whose event already passed turns into a Look.
  6. 6
    Per-part flags derive newparts.team and parts.create start on first visit to Agents and first open of the create form. Create stop 4 stays put on 409 AGENT_HANDLE_TAKEN until the handle is unique.
  7. 7
    Progress across devices backendlocalStorage is per browser, so a new laptop starts from stop 1. Syncing needs a tour field on /api/me (read and PATCH). Until then the key stays local.
