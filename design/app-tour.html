The first ten minutes, guided

An eleven-stop walk through truex for someone opening it for the first time. A soft spotlight picks out one surface at a time, a small coach card explains it in a sentence or two, and the tour runs a real demo request so helpers, the plan and insights fill in with live data. Three short agent tours follow the first time someone finds, creates or edits an agent.
