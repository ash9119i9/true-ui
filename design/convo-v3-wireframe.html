Overview

The page at a glance

One rule decides placement. Content (messages, work, answers and live requests) is inline in the stream. Status (turn state, goal, helpers) is pinned to the topbar, the goal strip and the session panel. Diagnostics (traces, tokens, limits) stay folded behind the receipt and the Insights tab. One derived turn state feeds every status surface, so they never disagree.
