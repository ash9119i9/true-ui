06 · Why now

SAP transformation can no longer wait.

SAP ECC customers face a fixed support deadline, and the move to SAP S/4HANA is complex, costly, and short on skilled people.

Where truex fits The slow, manual analysis, coding and testing at the heart of every migration. With approvals and execution evidence built in.

The deadline driving demand

  1. TodayMigration programs underway or still being plannedEvery ECC landscape must move, or pay to stay
  2. End of 2027SAP ECC 6.0 mainstream maintenance ends
  3. End of 2030Optional extended maintenance endsAvailable at additional cost

The destination is fixed. The capacity to get there is not.

01

Scarce SAP expertise

Experienced S/4HANA consultants and ABAP developers are in short supply, so programs slow down or stall.

02

Costly failed transformations

Overruns and troubled go-lives can cost millions and disrupt core business operations.

03

Compliance risk

Every code and configuration change must stay tested, approved, and auditable.

04

Manual, error-prone work

Custom-code analysis, remediation, and testing are still largely done by hand: slow, and easy to get wrong.

Maintenance dates refer to SAP's published commitments for SAP ECC 6.0 (EHP 6–8). Individual customer contracts and transition options may differ.
