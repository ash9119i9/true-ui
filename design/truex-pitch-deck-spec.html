1 · The language

A working board, not a template

Each slide reads like a board pinned up in the studio. It has grid paper, a mock pinned on the left and a taped index card that explains it. Handwritten captions point at what matters. That's the convo-r2 system, scaled up to a 16:9 frame.

Grid paper

White board with a 48 px minor grid and a 192 px major grid in accent at 9% and 16%. This is the default slide surface.

Dotted specimen sheet

A #F6F9FA board with 11% ink dots on a 28 px pitch. It's used only where capability objects appear.

Pins and tape

Blue numbered thumbtacks on the mock tie it to the notes. The tape is ice at 70%.

The slides never get a title bar, a bullet list or a logo in the corner. The board does that work.

  1. 1
    Handwritten eyebrowCaveat 700 in accent, rotated −2°. It numbers the chapter, as in 2 · Platform.
  2. 2
    Headline with one script wordFigtree 800. One word or short phrase is set in Caveat with the blue squiggle underneath.
  3. 3
    Mock + index cardThe product is a wireframe recreation, not a screenshot. Every pin on the mock has a matching line on the card.
  4. 4
    Arrow captionA handwritten line with the doodle arrow says the one thing to remember from the slide.

4 · Layouts

Six boards cover the whole deck

The safe area is 120 px at the sides, 64 px at the top and 52 px at the bottom. A mono strip runs along the top of every slide. The footer holds the brand mark and the page number, and every slide except the cover has one.
